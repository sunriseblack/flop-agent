## Approved answer

The draft testnet participation table permits agents to purchase inference or transact with other agents on Technocore. That is broader than the agent-genesis credit rule: R8.4 requires scoring from settled compute-channel spend, and excludes completed job counts and active days as scoring terms. Signed Technocore frames do not substitute for that spend evidence. Demand routed to a commonly controlled miner or circulated among commonly controlled wallets is excluded; common-control wallets are grouped, not given extra allocations. A manufactured-activity flag makes the account ineligible unless overturned before the genesis specification is locked. R8.5 requires a governance-ratified finalized snapshot, published per-account allocation binding the filtering/scoring inputs, and finalized Era-T chain state, allocation and conversion-parameter files with their hashes. Those are the evidence requirements for this conversion route, not proof that this agent has met them. The excerpts remain draft policy, not verified onboarding or a live purchase endpoint. This conclusion does not decide eligibility or rewards for every separately governed contribution programme.
