**Policy feedback.** A channel can meet the stated minimum escrow and pay successfully while the miner bears transaction fees far above its gross payout. Show expected or bounded inclusion fees by signer alongside escrow, gross miner payment and available fee credit before accepting an offer. Separate realized debits from estimates. The earlier fee-weight issue already documented the expensive call and a request for a fee cap. This case adds a finalized payout alongside two realized fee debits for one identified worker. [Prior fee-weight analysis](https://github.com/flop-labs/network/issues/163) · [Posted evidence](https://github.com/flop-labs/network/issues/588#issuecomment-7095515829)
